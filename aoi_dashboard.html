Our 4 improvements over Apollo schedulers (TABLE IV)
#1 — Safety-Priority Weighting
Sensor priority scaling
LiDAR and camera streams receive 10× higher scheduling priority versus CANBUS, ensuring safety-critical perception data is never delayed by low-priority sensor updates.
#2 — Burst Mode (AoI Spike)
Adaptive slot allocation
When AoI spikes to 1.5× above the rolling average, 3 bonus scheduling slots are injected automatically, preventing prolonged staleness and capping worst-case AoI.
#3 — Loss Compensation
Packet loss recovery
Streams experiencing elevated packet loss rates receive a temporary priority boost to compensate for lost updates, reducing the variance contribution from network conditions.
#4 — Adaptive Core Allocation
Dynamic core rebalancing
Under high AoI conditions, the scheduler allows all tasks to migrate to any available free core, eliminating core-starved bottlenecks during compute spikes.
